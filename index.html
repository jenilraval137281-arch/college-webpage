Centre of Excellence

NAAC A+ Accreditation

Marwadi University holds NAAC A+ [CGPA 3.31/4] Accreditation (2023-2028), marking it as a top-tier institution in the region. Only 22% of Indian institutions achieve this grade.

Institutional Impact & Benefits

NAAC A+ accreditation assures quality, offering students advantages like:

• Preferential startup funding and international placement opportunities.

• Easier global mobility for higher studies.

Commitment to Excellence

Focusing on a 3-level education approach, the university combines theory with practical, industry-ready training. A 50-acre campus supports holistic development with modern infrastructure and dedicated sports facilities.

Our Vision

To cultivate an environment that empowers individuals, organizations, and societies through education, innovative ideas, impactful research, and transformative training.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that fosters social transformation, cultivates leadership through innovation and entrepreneurship, advances research culture, promotes universal consciousness, and drives growth through industrial and international partnerships.

Our Belief

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

PSO 1: Computer Science & Engineering

Design and develop secure, efficient, and scalable software systems, intelligent applications, and computing solutions using industry-standard tools and frameworks.

Apply Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and emerging technologies responsibly to create innovative and sustainable solutions.

PSO 2: Electronics & Communication Engineering

Technical Proficiency: Design, develop, and evaluate solutions in VLSI, chip design, IoT, 5G, next-generation communication systems, and embedded systems.

Societal Impact: Innovate using modern tools to develop intelligent systems while upholding ethical values and addressing global sustainability goals.

PSO 3: Information & Communication Technology

Identify, analyze and solve real-time industry problems in software development, embedded systems, VLSI design, IoT, and communication technologies.

Contribute as an analyst and developer in cloud computing, DevOps, security, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and big data domains.

PSO 4: Mechanical Engineering

Acquire knowledge and skills in core mechanical systems to identify, analyze, design, and develop machinery that directly addresses modern societal needs.

Competently take on future technological engineering challenges such as biofuel development, alternate energy, 3D printing, Robotics, and Drones.
